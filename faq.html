HLTS Limited is a Lagos-based EdTech company that empowers schools and institutions across Africa with comprehensive digital learning platforms, student portals, and administrative tools tailored to modernize education.

To register your school, use the school registration form and share the details of your institution. The HLTS team can then follow up about onboarding.

HLTS@School is our comprehensive digital infrastructure designed specifically for primary and secondary schools. It includes tools for classroom management, student progress tracking, parent communication, and administrative compliance.

Access to the student portal is completely free for individual students whose schools or institutions have subscribed to the HLTS platform.

School onboarding typically takes between 1-2 weeks depending on the size of the institution and the amount of legacy data that needs to be transferred to our platform.

Our platform is fully responsive and web-based. It can be effortlessly accessed on desktop computers, laptops, tablets, and all modern smartphones.
